Pachetul `enigma2-hdsatelit` 0.6.20 pentru mașina `viper4k`, publicat ca actualizarea 8 a imaginii V10 0.6.6
(Meniu > Setări > Programe și actualizări > Actualizare software), e făcut din `enigma2/` de aici (comiterea 5777d8c48102cc315182a60984637acb9ec96ff9), în

actualizării 7 (eticheta `v0.6.19-v10`).

# HD Satelit 0.6.20 for the first Amiko Viper 4K (V10): the update from the menu

The `enigma2-hdsatelit` 0.6.20 package for the `viper4k` machine, published as update 8 of the V10 0.6.6 image (Menu >
Settings > Software & Firmware > Software update), is built from `enigma2/` here (commit 5777d8c48102cc315182a60984637acb9ec96ff9), in the build of the V10
r3 image: the `meta-local/` layer here, with the `enigma2-hdsatelit` recipe built with `SRCREV` = the commit above and
`PV = "0.6.20+git${SRCPV}"`. The skin (`skin/`) and the system packages stay those of update 7 (tag `v0.6.19-v10`).
