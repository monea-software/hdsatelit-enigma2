# HD Satelit 0.6.18 for the Octagon SF8008 and SF8008 MINI: update 6 from the menu

The `enigma2-hdsatelit` package for the `sf8008` and `sf8008m` machines, published as update 6 of the 0.6.13 image (Menu >
Settings > Software & Firmware > Software update), is built from `enigma2/` here (commit d2dc822e2a52e13dfa5516c14b5a8f2f7eff30c6), in the 0.6.13
image build of each machine: the `meta-local/` layer here (the image's, unchanged), with the `enigma2-hdsatelit` recipe
built with `SRCREV` = the commit above and `PV = "0.6.18+git${SRCPV}"`. The skin (`skin/`) and the other packages are
those of the 0.6.13 image.
